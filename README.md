## The word

**Discontinued**

The ex as a product line that has been discontinued: no longer made, no longer stocked, no restock coming. The whole model was pulled from the shelves — not recalled for a fix, not on sale, just gone from the catalogue.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes the single word `Discontinued` — 12 characters, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`, then reads it back and asserts it is exactly that word and passes the 1–111 character, non-whitespace gate.

## Why this word

See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the local judging of 12 candidate words against the published judge brief (verdict on an ex first, then comic punch, surprise, craft, recognition). "Discontinued" won: it is unmistakably a verdict on a former partner, it lands the product-line joke at a glance, it is fresher than the field's saturated "Recalled" / "Unsubscribed" family, it is one clean correctly-spelled word, and anyone who has ever seen a "discontinued" label recognises the truth in it.
